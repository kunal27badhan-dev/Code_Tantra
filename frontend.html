The Problem

Why New Contributors Give Up Before They Start

😤

The Struggle is Real

  • ❌Thousands of issues with no useful filtering
  • ❌"good-first-issue" label is inconsistently applied
  • ❌Complexity far exceeds beginner skill level
  • ❌Stale issues that are already fixed or abandoned
  • ❌No way to match issues to your language/skill set
  • ❌Overwhelming without context or mentorship signals
✨

IssueQuest's Solution

  • ✅Smart multi-dimensional filtering by skill & language
  • ✅Freshness signals — only active, recent issues
  • ✅Community-rated difficulty scores beyond just labels
  • ✅Mentor-available filter to find guided opportunities
  • ✅Save & track issues across multiple sessions
  • ✅AI-matched suggestions based on your profile
